##

Multi-function Printers

To provide convenient printing to the campus community, we offer multi-function printers and have set up printout pick-up points around campus. Available printing services include black and white, color, and A3 and A4 paper size options.

Note: Multiple pages per sheet is not supported by iOS, Android, ChromOS and Web Print.

The default setting is to print on both sides , but you can change this setting from the software or the printer properties.

Charges

Print jobs are charged to your print budget at the same rate as computer barns.  Please refer to the ITSO Printing Facilities for details.

Reporting Problems

If there is a paper jam or the printer supplies have run out, please contact the student helper in the computer barn or email us. Don't attempt to fix it yourself.

Other Tips

Only print what's necessary. Consider the environmental cost before printing and try to find alternative, eco-friendly solutions.
